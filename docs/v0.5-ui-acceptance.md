# Pocam v0.5 — design target and release gate

**Release state: not approved for production.** A successful Android CI compilation is not a UI, camera, or performance acceptance test. The supplied Pro Mode reference is the visual target; the current screenshots do not establish a pixel-perfect match.

## Fixed visual language
- Colors: black, graphite gray, brushed silver, copper orange `#FF7A00`.
- No wallpaper/system-derived accent colors.
- Flat, straight-edged translucent graphite camera header (NO rounded bottom corners), compact circular icons, white text, orange active controls.
- Android status, navigation, and cutout safe areas must remain usable.

## Camera controls
- Live preview visible above the control deck.
- Curved zoom ruler and curved five-mode selector share no overlapping space.
- Only the curved selector changes PHOTO, VIDEO, NIGHT, PRO, PORTRAIT modes.
- Shutter performs capture or video start/stop only; long press has no mode wheel.
- Focus: tap preview to focus, hold preview to lock focus.
- Expanded header: quick torch/grid/timer/exposure controls.

## Pro mode
- Collapsed right-side rail: ISO, Shutter, White Balance, Focus, EV.
- Tap any parameter to open a small tray just above the shutter.
- Only one parameter appears at a time and the live preview remains visible.
- Expanded tray replaces the mode ribbon temporarily; collapse to switch modes.
- Zoom remains accessible; shutter and gallery/flip buttons remain accessible.
- Manual options must be sensor-supported, otherwise display unavailable.

## Settings
- Tabs: Capture, Camera, Video, Appearance.
- Functional: grid, guide, timer, torch, shutter sound, exposure, video quality,
  video stabilization request, header opacity.
- Device-managed: JPEG, photo resolution, frame rate, image processing.
- Future features (RAW / histogram / peaking / zebra) must be marked planned;
  the UI must not falsely claim they work.

## Remaining device acceptance
- Install the new v0.5 APK on Motorola Edge 60 Pro.
- Verify no status-bar/header overlap on launch or settings.
- Verify Pro rail/tray do not cover zoom or shutter.
- Confirm the selected curved pill follows the active mode while tapping.
- Confirm capture, video, Gallery, editor export and focus lock work on device.
- Confirm the fixed orange color remains unchanged when wallpaper theme changes.
- CI green verifies compilation and APK publication, not device rendering.

## Required UI and performance acceptance (real devices)
- Compare a screenshot of each UI state against the provided reference at equivalent logical viewport sizes: header, PRO badge, rail, compact tray, curved zoom, mode selector and shutter. Check both portrait and landscape.
- Settings must open and close immediately without a full-screen slide or crossfade; tabs should switch without animating two content trees.
- Record at least 30 open/close and tab-change repetitions on Motorola Edge 60 Pro. The Settings transition must have no visually obvious freeze or black preview. Record measured p95 input-to-render latency; target <100 ms.
- Profile the **release** build (not just a debuggable APK) using Android Studio/Perfetto FrameTimeline. Target at least 95% of UI frames within the display's 60 Hz frame budget (16.7 ms) under normal lighting and no long main-thread stalls. Report actual results, not estimates.
- Test Pro ISO, shutter, focus, WB and EV against the lens's actual Camera2 capabilities; controls must not display AUTO when auto-exposure has been disabled underneath.
- Confirm tap-to-focus, focus lock, zoom, front/rear switching, gallery preview, save, export, and Video 720p/1080p/4K fallback in repeated runs. Verify video audio expectations and permission handling before release.
- Night/Portrait currently run on the same ordinary photo pipeline while OEM extensions are disabled. Either implement and verify their real capture processing or label/disable them as previews before production.
- Add instrumentation/screenshot tests and at least one additional Android device for compatibility. Do not call v0.5 production-ready based on compile success alone.

## Settings responsiveness regression gate
- Settings toggle rows change their visual On/Off state immediately (no animated Material3 thumb delay); the entire row is a touch target with Switch semantics for accessibility.
- Video resolution/stabilization selections update requested values immediately but never rebind the camera while Settings is visible. Apply at most one pending rebind after leaving Settings and only when not recording.
- Do not persist header-opacity changes on every slider drag event; apply on drag completion.
- Validate repeated grid, center guide, sound, torch and stabilization changes in a physical Android 16 device. Watch for stutters or missed taps under ongoing camera preview.
- CI includes a **separate optimized benchmark APK**, signed only with a debug test key and using a distinct package name `com.pocam.camera.benchmark`. It must **never** be treated as a Play Store artifact. Verify real frame pacing on this non-debuggable R8-optimized variant.
- Compare reference screenshot with real device screenshot, including exact edge geometry (especially flat header), rail, tray and thumb-size / selected-pill positions. Pixel-perfect approval must be based on screenshots; passing CI alone is not visual approval.

## Pocam signature motion — lightweight and optional
- The five-mode selector orange capsule glides along its cached Bézier curve over ~190ms. No full-screen crossfade, CameraX restart, or additional frame processing is triggered for the animation.
- The shutter's silver center scales to 90% then springs back while the outer touch target remains stationary.
- The focus reticle expands from 82% to full size for ~175ms when a focus action occurs.
- Pro rail selection glows briefly; the expand/collapse chevron rotates without animating the tray's layout or the camera surface.
- Settings switches translate only the 24dp thumb over ~145ms and tabs subtly transition their selected background over ~120ms. Switching pages stays instant.
- Settings → Appearance → Pocam motion is persisted under `motion_enabled` (default on), and disables all new micro-animations immediately.
- Validate motion on the R8-optimized Pocam Benchmark build with 20 cycles each of mode selection, shutter, tap-to-focus and Settings switches. Ensure the preview does not stutter. Reduced-motion system animation duration settings should also be respected by Compose.
- No continuous looping animation, blur effect, per-frame object allocation, or camera use-case rebinding is introduced for decorative effects.
